# Sentiment labelling guidelines (example project)

## Labels
Use exactly one label per text: positive, negative, neutral or mixed.
Label the sentiment the writer expresses, not the topic. Do not invent other labels.

## Sarcasm and irony
When the wording is positive but the intent is clearly negative, label the intended sentiment.
Explain the sarcasm in the rationale and quote the words that reveal it.

## Mixed sentiment
Use mixed only when the text contains clear positive and clear negative statements that both matter.
A single complaint inside an otherwise positive text is still positive.

## Rationale requirements
Every label needs a rationale of at least one full sentence.
The rationale must quote the words from the text that decided the label.
A rationale such as "positive" or "looks fine" is not accepted and must be corrected.

## Personal data
Never copy names, e-mail addresses or phone numbers into a rationale.
Replace them with [redacted] before submitting.

## Unreadable or ambiguous text
If the text is unreadable, label it neutral and write "unreadable" in the rationale.
If it is ambiguous, pick the most likely sentiment and explain the doubt.
